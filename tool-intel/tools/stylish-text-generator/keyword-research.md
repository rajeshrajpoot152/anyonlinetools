## 3. High-Value Search Intent Breakdown

1. **Transactional / Action Intent ("Copy and Paste"):**
   - Users do NOT want to download `.ttf` or `.otf` font files to install on Windows.
   - They want **Unicode characters** that can be copied with 1 tap and pasted anywhere (Instagram bio, WhatsApp message, Free Fire name, Twitter handle, TikTok profile).
   - *Technical Requirement:* Every font card must have an instantaneous 1-click **Copy** button with animated checkmark feedback.
2. **Instant Live Typing ("Type once, preview all styles"):**
   - User types their name or sentence once in the input box, and the tool immediately transforms and renders it across 50+ aesthetic unicode font styles simultaneously.
3. **Filter & Category Tabs:**
   - Tabs needed: `All`, `Popular / Viral`, `Cursive & Script`, `Gothic / Bold`, `Aesthetic / Cute`, `Gaming (FF/BGMI)`, `Decorations & Wings`, `Glitched / Zalgo`.
4. **Symbol & Nickname Decorator:**
   - Gamers and Instagram bio creators love surrounding their name with wings, swords, crowns, hearts, and stars:
     - Examples: `꧁༺ ₦Ї₦ℑ₳ ༻꧂`, `★彡[ᴅᴇᴀᴅʟʏ]彡★`, `♛ KING ♛`, `✿ 𝒱𝒶𝓁𝑒𝓃𝓉𝒾𝓃𝒶 ✿`.
