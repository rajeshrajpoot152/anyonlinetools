### Key Strategic Takeaways:
1. **The `youtube font` Opportunity:**
   - Content creators search for stylish fonts to make YouTube video titles stand out in the algorithm feed or style their YouTube channel "About" descriptions.
   - *Action for AnyOnlineTools:* Add YouTube to our supported platforms matrix and mention "YouTube Title & Channel Font Generator" in our semantic content.

   - Users look for wavy/curved text (`〰️`, `~`, wavy diacritics). Our tool will include a dedicated wavy/curved text style.
3. **The Italian Heart Query (`font con simbolo cuore`):**
   - Italian users search for "fonts with heart symbol".
   - Our Italian translation (`/it/`) will explicitly cover `font con simbolo cuore` and heart decorators (`♡ ... ♡`).
