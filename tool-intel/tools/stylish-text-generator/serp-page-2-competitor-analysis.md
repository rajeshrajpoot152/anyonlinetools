## 2. Critical Discoveries from Page 2

### 1. The WhatsApp Dedicated Market (`whatsappfonts.com`):
- WhatsApp users have distinct formatting needs:
  - Bold, Italic, and Strikethrough are built-in (`*text*`, `_text_`, `~text~`), but users want **Cursive, Bubble numbers, Small Caps, and Wing decorations** that WhatsApp doesn't provide.
  - *Action for AnyOnlineTools:* A dedicated section explaining "How to send Stylish Fonts in WhatsApp" with a **1-tap "Share to WhatsApp"** button.

### 2. Standalone Value of `small-text-generator` (`dnschecker.org`):
- `Small Text Generator` has enough independent search demand that major network utility sites like DNS Checker build and rank dedicated sub-pages for it.
- *Action for AnyOnlineTools:* Our Small Caps (`ꜱᴍᴀʟʟ ᴄᴀᴘꜱ`) and Superscript (`ˢᵐᵃˡˡ`) styles must be prominently featured in their own quick filter tab.

### 3. Title Tag Evolution (Emoji & Bracket CTR Hack):
Competitors on Page 2 use even more aggressive symbols to compete for clicks:
- `【𝕮𝖔𝖕𝖞 ⓐⓝⓓ 𝕻𝖆𝖘𝖙𝖊】`
- `✓ 𝓒𝓸𝓹𝔂 & 𝓟𝓪𝓼𝓽𝓮`
- `⚡ 𝓢𝓽𝔂𝓵𝓲𝓼𝓱 𝓚𝓮𝔂𝓫𝓸𝓪𝓻𝓭 ➜ ⚡`
- `➜#1 👑 Copy ⓐⓝⓓ Paste`

We will balance high CTR Unicode styling in our Title tag without triggering Google's title truncation or spam filters:
> `Stylish Text Generator (𝓬𝓸𝓹𝔂 𝓪𝓷𝓭 𝓹𝓪𝓼𝓽𝓮) ➜ 100+ Cool Fonts & Nicknames`
